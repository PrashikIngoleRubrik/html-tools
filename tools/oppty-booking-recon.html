What gets compared

  • Header sync (20 fields) – close date, type, sub-type, deal reg, split, partner, owner, secondary/renewal owner, SE, SDR led, SE misaligned, strategic account, alliance partners, ISR/SDR credit, original lead owner, R2K, PO accepted timestamp. The batch only copies them while the booking's Field Update Status is blank (not frozen).
  • Line level – each opportunity product × (order qty ÷ opp qty) vs its booking line: ACV, New/Expansion, Subsumed, Renewal ACV/TCV, product-type ACV, discounts. Net = Gross × (100 − rebate%).
  • Totals – opportunity ACV/TCV fields vs the sum of booking lines (and vs the sum of opportunity lines), to separate opportunity-side issues from booking sync issues.
  • Theatre – opportunity ETM Theatre/Area/Region/Territory vs booking split.

Differences of 0.01 or less are treated as rounding. Blank amounts are treated as 0. The root-cause notes are indicators – confirm with the extra queries suggested in the report.

Booking objects are restricted for Claude's Salesforce connector, so queries are run by you in production. Nothing is sent anywhere by this page.
